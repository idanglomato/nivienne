What's Included

Everything Your Love Story
Deserves

Every Nivienne & Co. invitation is a complete experience — beautifully crafted, thoughtfully engineered, and delivered with grace.

RSVP & Guest Wishes

Guests confirm attendance and leave heartfelt messages in a single seamless flow. No third-party forms, no broken links — just grace.

Live Countdown Timer

A real-time countdown to your ceremony, building anticipation and keeping the sacred date alive in every guest's mind.

Photo Gallery

Curate a gallery of your most precious pre-wedding moments. Full-bleed, swipeable, and beautifully composed — every image, a memory.

Ambient Background Music

Set the emotional tone before a single word is read. Choose a piece of music that speaks your love — guests toggle it with one quiet tap.

Venue Maps & Navigation

One tap takes guests directly to turn-by-turn directions. Embedded maps with custom venue pins ensure no one arrives late to your sacred moment.

Save the Date Integration

Guests add your wedding date directly to their phone calendar with a single tap — synced with native iOS and Android calendar apps.

Love Story Timeline

Tell the story of how you found each other — from first glance to forever. A visual, scroll-through narrative your guests will remember.

Pre-wedding Video

Embed your cinematic pre-wedding film directly in the invitation. Let guests feel the emotion before they even arrive at your celebration.

Digital Gift Confirmation

Guests confirm monetary gifts elegantly online. You receive a live dashboard of who has sent what — graceful, discreet, and organised.

QR Code Check-in

Each invitation carries a unique QR code. Guests are checked in instantly at the door — modern, professional, and deeply personal.

Invitation Tracking

See who has opened your invitation, when they opened it, and from which link. Know your guests are informed — in real time.

Unlimited Personalised Sharing

Each guest receives a link addressed to them by name. Share via text, WhatsApp, or email — infinitely, without restriction.
